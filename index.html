About Linchpin Connect
"We connect people, ideas, and impact — across cultures, sectors, and hemispheres."

Linchpin Connect is a strategic consultancy founded on two decades of international experience building mission-driven communities, managing impact ventures, and fostering cross-cultural partnerships across four continents. Founded and led by Anna-Corina Groot — half Dutch, half Kiwi, and a truly global citizen — the consultancy brings creativity, warmth, and rigorous strategic thinking to every engagement.

Based in Tāmaki Makaurau / Auckland, our team works globally, regularly engaging partners across Australia, Asia, Europe, and the Americas. We are as comfortable in boardrooms in Singapore and programming impact summits in Sydney as in producing theatre in community halls in West Auckland or on film sets in Tāmaki.

Māori values of kaitiakitanga, manaakitanga, and rangatiratanga form the foundation of how we lead and work — embedded in our governance through Anna's role as Chair of EcoMatters, and carried into every project engagement in Aotearoa and abroad.
